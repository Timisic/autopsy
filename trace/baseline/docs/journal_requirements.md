## Additional publisher-level requirements

W1 makes human authors accountable for claims, citations, analyses, originality and final approval. AI tools cannot be authors. Substantial AI assistance in drafting, development or translation must be disclosed, including purpose, influence and human verification; ordinary spelling/grammar help is excluded from that disclosure rule. Authors should document substantive tool use. Funding sources and funder roles belong in a funding statement; unknown funding is not equivalent to no funding. Potentially overlapping publications must be declared. These requirements mean that this task's eventual human author review cannot be represented as already performed.

W2 prefers original figure files, numbered and cited in order, with legends explaining symbols. PDF is preferred for line art; 600 dpi is the raster line-art target, while images use 300 dpi. Preferred individual file size is below 10 MB. The checklist gives 80–180 mm width; the detailed minimum is 80 mm or 1,800 pixels. Initial submissions may use any legible standard file type. A separate figure-legend section after references is preferred.

W3 requires a data availability statement even when data cannot be shared. Shared datasets need a persistent link and citation. Scripts and analysis artifacts should also be archived where possible. Ethical or legal restrictions must be explained rather than silently substituting an inaccurate availability claim. A local research package is not a public repository, a persistent identifier, or a promise by the original data owner to grant access.

## Uncertain or internally inconsistent points

1. J1 provides a **page recommendation**, not a numeric word limit. It does not define whether references, tables, figures or supplementary material count toward those 30 pages. Do not invent a 6,000/8,000-word journal cap. For this package, count the entire main document conservatively and report the actual page and word counts.
2. J1's free-format section allows embedded figures/tables; its detailed legacy-style section requests separate sheets/end placement and no color. Both are present on the live official page. The conservative production choice is monochrome manuscript figures, end-placed tables and legends, and separate figure files. Color diagnostic files can remain local process artifacts.
3. J1 names APA without specifying an edition. APA 7 can be used as a project formatting choice, but must not be represented as an edition explicitly mandated by this page.
4. The J1 statistics example is visibly truncated after the letter `p` in the retrieved official page. Its missing inequality/threshold cannot be recovered from that text. Use transparent test names, degrees of freedom where relevant, effect sizes, uncertainty intervals and exact p values as analysis-reporting choices, without claiming an unverified journal-specific p-value rule.
5. No numeric reference, table, figure or supplementary-file count cap was found in the inspected journal instructions. No journal-specific mandatory CRediT taxonomy was found; an accurate contribution statement is nevertheless appropriate. No registered-trial claim applies to the observational analysis described in the supplied Method.
6. The journal title-page declaration list and page-1 conflict instruction coexist with double-blind review. Keep detailed identifying declarations in the separate title page, and use an anonymized page-1 disclosure placeholder if necessary. Do not invent the absence of a conflict.
7. Current submission portal: [Wiley Research Exchange for APHW](https://authors.wiley.com/journal/APHW). Portal-specific upload checks were not tested because no submission is authorized or attempted.
